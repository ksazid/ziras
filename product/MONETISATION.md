# Ziras Monetisation Model

Status: **Approved and locked**
Approved by: Product owner (`ksazid`)
Approved at: 2026-08-31T23:21:00+02:00

## Principle

Ziras consumer discovery remains free. Merchants do not need to pay or onboard to be discovered. Monetisation is layered on top of measurable value generated by Ziras rather than controlling organic inclusion.

## Locked commercial stack

### 1. Affiliate / transaction commission

Primary transaction-linked revenue stream.

Ziras may earn a referral or affiliate fee when a user completes a measurable downstream action such as:

- booking;
- ordering;
- purchasing;
- ticket purchase;
- voucher purchase or redemption where permitted;
- another attributable conversion supported by the destination provider.

This does not require merchants to pay for organic discovery inclusion.

### 2. Sponsored discoveries

Merchants may pay to increase visibility of an existing legitimate discovery.

Rules:

- sponsored placement must be clearly labelled;
- sponsorship must not bypass Ziras relevance, freshness or policy checks;
- possible commercial models include CPC, CPA or fixed campaign budget;
- organic discovery remains available independently of sponsorship.

### 3. Performance-based merchant campaigns

Merchants may run outcome-oriented campaigns through Ziras and pay for measurable results such as:

- booking;
- redemption;
- qualified lead;
- attributable purchase;
- another agreed conversion event.

Example use cases include filling low-demand restaurant tables, spa capacity, activity inventory or other time-sensitive local availability.

### 4. Merchant Pro

Optional paid merchant product. Merchant Pro is not required for a business to appear in Ziras organic discovery.

Potential capabilities:

- claim business;
- create and manage offers;
- offer and discovery performance analytics;
- competitor and local deal intelligence;
- AI recommendations;
- campaign tooling;
- conversion and attribution reporting.

Pricing is intentionally not locked yet.

### 5. Corporate Premium — Ziras Benefits

A B2B employee-benefits product sold to employers.

Working product name: **Ziras Benefits**.

Potential capabilities:

- employee-only or employer-funded offers;
- local dining benefits;
- family and kids benefits;
- activities and entertainment benefits;
- spa and wellness benefits;
- grocery and household savings;
- company-funded vouchers or rewards where later supported;
- personalised employee discovery feed;
- aggregated employer reporting on adoption, engagement and estimated savings.

Employer reporting must remain aggregated and must not expose sensitive individual behaviour.

Commercial model may use company subscription, per-employee pricing or enterprise contract pricing. Exact pricing is intentionally not locked yet.

## Deferred / not-priority monetisation

### Consumer Premium

Deferred. Ziras should not depend on consumer subscription revenue for the initial commercial model.

A future consumer subscription may be evaluated only after strong free-user value and retention are demonstrated.

### Generic banner advertising

Not part of the preferred monetisation model. Avoid unless a future product decision explicitly reopens it.

## Locked order of commercial development

1. Free consumer discovery
2. Affiliate / transaction monetisation
3. Sponsored discoveries and performance campaigns
4. Merchant Pro
5. Corporate Premium / Ziras Benefits
6. Consumer Premium only if later evidence supports it

## Product guardrails

- No merchant onboarding or payment is required for organic discovery.
- Paid placement must be clearly distinguishable from organic recommendations.
- Payment must not override source policy, freshness, relevance or trust controls.
- Ziras should favour revenue tied to measurable user or merchant outcomes over generic impression-based advertising.
- The 14-day Malta discovery-engine POC remains a consumer-value validation exercise; this monetisation decision does not change VS-07 success gates, runtime scope, source permissions, release state or production-enable state.
