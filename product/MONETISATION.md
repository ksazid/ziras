# Ziras Monetisation Model

Status: **Approved and locked as future option set**
Approved by: Product owner (`ksazid`)
Updated at: 2026-08-31T23:21:00+02:00

## Launch and monetisation principle

Ziras launches and operates as a **free consumer discovery product**.

Monetisation is **not part of the POC** and is **not part of the initial public release**.

The product should focus first on user value, growth, retention and discovery quality. Monetisation should be reconsidered only when Ziras reaches approximately **10,000 users**, and even then only if monetisation is required and the product has sufficient evidence to choose the right model.

Merchants do not need to pay or onboard to be discovered organically.

## Monetisation trigger

Before approximately 10,000 users:

- consumer access remains free;
- no monetisation requirement;
- no paid merchant dependency;
- no monetisation changes to ranking or organic inclusion;
- primary objective is product-market validation and user growth.

At approximately 10,000 users:

- review retention, engagement, discovery quality and operating cost;
- determine whether monetisation is required;
- validate demand from merchants and employers;
- select one or more monetisation models from the approved future option set below;
- require a separate product approval before implementation or activation.

Reaching 10,000 users is a **review trigger, not an automatic monetisation switch**.

## Approved future monetisation option set

### 1. Affiliate / transaction commission

Ziras may later earn a referral or affiliate fee when a user completes a measurable downstream action such as:

- booking;
- ordering;
- purchasing;
- ticket purchase;
- voucher purchase or redemption where permitted;
- another attributable conversion supported by the destination provider.

Organic discovery remains free and independent of affiliate participation.

### 2. Sponsored discoveries

Merchants may later pay to increase visibility of an existing legitimate discovery.

Rules:

- sponsored placement must be clearly labelled;
- sponsorship must not bypass Ziras relevance, freshness or policy checks;
- possible models include CPC, CPA or fixed campaign budget;
- organic discovery remains available independently of sponsorship.

### 3. Performance-based merchant campaigns

Merchants may later run outcome-oriented campaigns and pay for measurable results such as:

- booking;
- redemption;
- qualified lead;
- attributable purchase;
- another agreed conversion event.

Potential use cases include filling low-demand restaurant tables, spa capacity, activity inventory or other time-sensitive local availability.

### 4. Merchant Pro

Optional future paid merchant product. Merchant Pro is never required for a business to appear in organic Ziras discovery.

Potential capabilities:

- claim business;
- create and manage offers;
- offer and discovery performance analytics;
- competitor and local deal intelligence;
- AI recommendations;
- campaign tooling;
- conversion and attribution reporting.

Pricing is intentionally not locked.

### 5. Corporate Premium — Ziras Benefits

Optional future B2B employee-benefits product sold to employers.

Working product name: **Ziras Benefits**.

Potential capabilities:

- employee-only or employer-funded offers;
- local dining benefits;
- family and kids benefits;
- activities and entertainment benefits;
- spa and wellness benefits;
- grocery and household savings;
- company-funded vouchers or rewards where later supported;
- personalised employee discovery feed;
- aggregated employer reporting on adoption, engagement and estimated savings.

Employer reporting must remain aggregated and must not expose sensitive individual behaviour.

Potential commercial models include company subscription, per-employee pricing or enterprise contracts. Exact pricing is intentionally not locked.

## Deferred / not-priority monetisation

### Consumer Premium

Deferred. Ziras should not depend on consumer subscription revenue.

A future consumer subscription may be evaluated only after strong free-user value and retention are demonstrated and only through a separate product decision.

### Generic banner advertising

Not part of the preferred monetisation model. Avoid unless a future product decision explicitly reopens it.

## Future commercial priority order

If monetisation is later required, evaluate in this order:

1. Affiliate / transaction commission
2. Sponsored discoveries and performance-based merchant campaigns
3. Merchant Pro
4. Corporate Premium / Ziras Benefits
5. Consumer Premium only if later evidence supports it

This ordering is a future evaluation priority, not a release roadmap or commitment to activate every model.

## Product guardrails

- Initial Ziras release is free.
- Monetisation is not part of the Malta POC.
- Approximately 10,000 users is the earliest agreed review point, not an automatic paywall or monetisation event.
- Any monetisation activation requires a separate product decision and approval.
- No merchant onboarding or payment is required for organic discovery.
- Paid placement, if introduced later, must be clearly distinguishable from organic recommendations.
- Payment must never override source policy, freshness, relevance or trust controls.
- The 14-day Malta discovery-engine POC remains solely a consumer-value validation exercise; this decision does not change VS-07 gates, runtime scope, source permissions, release state or production-enable state.
